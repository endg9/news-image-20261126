# Source notes — The World in Pictures, 26 November 2026

## What this file does
This file traces every fact in the picture back to where it was first published.
It lets any reader re-check all 26 items instead of taking the image on trust.
The picture squeezes one day of world news into a single square image.
Nothing in it dates from any day other than Thursday 26 November 2026.

## How the day was defined
The day runs from 17:00 UTC on 25 November to 17:00 UTC on 26 November.
That span is the Jakarta day, seven hours ahead of UTC.
A story counts only when its first report falls inside that span.
Follow-ups to older news do not count, however prominent they are.
Each note below gives the first-report time, the outlet, and the check done.
